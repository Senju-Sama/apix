The Macroeconomic Stakes

The Reserve Bank of India is Setting Monetary Policy Blindfolded.

Under India's Flexible Inflation Targeting framework, the RBI Monetary Policy Committee is mandated to keep inflation at 4% (±2%).

When airfare inflation is underreported or lagged by weeks, the Transport Subgroup of the Consumer Price Index (CPI) becomes inaccurate—leading to delayed or mistaken interest rate decisions.

! The Problem isn't booking tickets. The Problem is National Macroeconomic Intelligence.
