Layer 2 • Data Quality Guardian

The 15 Aviation Edge Cases Gauntlet

pipeline/models.py & sentinel.py

Comparing raw checkout prices causes false inflation alarms. Every incoming quote passes through 4 strict audit checkpoints:

1. Pure Economic Fare Unbundling

EC-1 & EC-3: We isolate pure Base Fare + Fuel (YQ). We strip User Development Fees (UDF), PSF, GST (5%), and OTA convenience drip fees (MMT ₹499 vs EMT ₹0).

Economic Fare = Final Fare - (Taxes + Drip Fee)

2. 'Lite' Fare Harmonization

EC-2: 7kg cabin-only "Lite" fares (e.g., IndiGo R07LGT) are ₹450 cheaper than standard 15kg economy. Comparing them triggers false +15.7% inflation spikes. We normalize all to 15kg standard.

If FareBasis == 'LGT' ➔ Harmonize (+₹450)

3. IQR Outlier Bounds & T+1 Bias

EC-7 & EC-5: Bounded between ₹1,500 and ₹35,000 to filter glitch fares and business-class leaks. When cheap seats sell out on T+1, hedonic carry-forward prevents artificial surges.

[max(1500, Q1 - 1.5*IQR), min(35000, Q3 + 2.5*IQR)]

4. Timezone, Paise & Route Filters

EC-8, 10, 11: Hard filter on non-stop direct flights only. Enforces Asia/Kolkata on midnight red-eyes and rescales integer paise quotes (Cleartrip 450000 ➔ 4500).

is_non_stop = True & timezone = 'Asia/Kolkata'
